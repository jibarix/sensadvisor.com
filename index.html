01 / Principles

Sixsides

People, processes, technology, data, analytics, and governance have to move together.

FPeople

People know where the work bends and breaks. Their judgment belongs in the design.

RProcesses

Automation works best after the process makes sense. A faster bad process is still a bad process.

BTechnology

Systems work better when they connect cleanly. Data should move without constant repair.

UData

Good decisions need data people can find, understand, and trust. Clear definitions matter as much as clean records.

DAnalytics

Analytics should make a decision clearer. The method should fit the question, and the result should be explainable.

LGovernance

Clear rules make responsible use easier. People should know who decides, who owns, and who checks.

03 / About

Jorge A. Arroyo

After a decade in finance, I got tired of seeing important decisions made with disconnected data. So I started building the bridge myself.

Today, I design and build business systems for planning, analysis, operations, and decision support. My work combines xP&A, financial modeling, and business valuation with data engineering, business intelligence, and custom application development. That combination allows me to approach problems from both the business and technical sides.

I work from the problem outward: understanding the process, determining how information should flow, designing the appropriate system, and leading or carrying out the implementation.

Since 2022, I have also pursued independent research into AI and large language models, focusing on their practical capabilities, limitations, and appropriate use in business. That work informs realistic assessments of where AI can help, where other approaches are more appropriate, and what implementation risks need to be considered.

What the symbols mean

The cube starts scrambled and solves itself as you scroll. These symbols describe its position and its moves.

g ∈ 𝒢, |𝒢| = 43,252,003,274,489,856,000
A cube has about 43 quintillion possible positions. 𝒢 means all of them; g is this page’s starting scramble.
𝒰 𝒟 ℛ ℒ ℱ ℬ
The six faces: Up, Down, Right, Left, Front and Back. A letter on its own means: turn that face a quarter turn clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it.
ℛ2 ℛ−1
2 means a half turn, counted as one move. −1 means a quarter turn the other way (counter-clockwise).
g = …
The scramble: the 20 random moves that mixed up this cube.
g−1 = …
The solve: the same 20 moves undone, last one first, each turned back the other way. A half turn undoes itself, so it stays the same.
Turn 01/20
The move playing now, which is also highlighted. At the end it shows 20/20 and e.
Section headers
The moves that play while you read that section.
g · g−1 = e
Scramble, then undo it, and the cube is solved again. e means “solved”.
↻
Picks a new random scramble. Your place on the page stays the same.

Any cube position can be solved in 20 moves or fewer, counting a half turn as one move. This page simply undoes its scramble, so its solve usually isn’t the shortest.
